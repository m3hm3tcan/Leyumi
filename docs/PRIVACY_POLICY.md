# Leyumi Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Leyumi is an offline baby-care application. This version does not require an account and does not send care records to Leyumi servers.

## Data used by the app

Leyumi stores the child profiles, birth dates, growth measurements, feeding and diaper records, milk inventory, care plans and application preferences entered by the user.

## Local storage

Records are stored only in Leyumi's private application storage on the user's device. Cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this version. Removing the application, clearing its storage or losing the device may permanently remove these records.

## Collection and sharing

Leyumi does not transmit, sell or share care records with the developer or third parties. A report leaves the application only when the user explicitly invokes the device's share or print controls and chooses a destination.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the user deletes them, resets Leyumi, clears application storage or uninstalls the application. The **Reset Leyumi** control in Settings permanently deletes all locally stored Leyumi data.

## Security

Leyumi relies on Android's application sandbox and the user's device lock to protect local records. Users should keep their devices updated and protected with a PIN, password or biometric lock.
